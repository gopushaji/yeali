Our Story

Made by hand,
led by heart.

Yeali began at a single sewing table, with founder and designer Vidhu cutting, draping and stitching every piece herself. What started as a passion shared over Instagram grew into a community of women who wanted clothing that felt personal — not mass-produced.

Every Yeali garment is designed by Vidhu and made in small batches with natural, breathable fabrics. We don't chase trends or seasons. We make clothes meant to be worn, loved and kept.

“I design each piece the way I'd make it for a friend — with patience, and with love.”— Vidhu, Founder & Designer
